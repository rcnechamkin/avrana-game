# Mission & Objectives

## Mission — LOCKED

The **Mission** is the public, fixed goal of the selected Scenario.

Properties:

* everyone knows it;
* it is selected before play;
* it does not change mid-session;
* it gives the game a stable shared center.

### Standard Scenario

> Ensure the ship reaches its destination.

This is the MVP Mission.

## Primary Objective — LOCKED

The **Primary Objective** is a participant's actual personal victory condition.

For most players at the start of Standard play:

> Ensure the ship reaches its destination.

Mission and Primary are often identical at first, but remain separate concepts.

## Divergent Primaries — PROVISIONAL

Some sessions may give a player a lore-backed Primary that diverges from the Mission.

Examples:

* Ensure Player 2 dies before arrival.
* Destroy the AI at all costs.
* Secure classified data and escape alive.
* Preserve the AI on portable storage and escape.
* Ensure only you and the AI survive.
* Destroy the ship.

Do not frame these as generic "evil traitor" roles. Give them an in-world reason.

## Objective change — LOCKED

A Primary may change through conscious player choice.

Examples:

* accepting an AI bargain;
* accepting formal recruitment;
* knowingly defecting from the Mission.

If a choice changes victory conditions, the UI must say so explicitly.

## Recruited vs. manipulated — LOCKED

**Recruited/aligned:** the player knowingly accepted a new objective or alliance.

**Manipulated:** the player still has their original objective but was tricked into helping another actor.

## Secondary Objectives — LOCKED

Meaningful optional goals that affect score/narrative but do not independently determine victory.

## Tertiary Objectives — LOCKED

Smaller optional accomplishments/challenges.

### PROVISIONAL objective budget

* 1 Primary
* 0–2 Secondary
* 0–3 Tertiary

## No guaranteed hostile human — LOCKED

Valid sessions include:

* all humans initially aligned;
* one divergent human;
* two divergent humans;
* initially aligned players who later defect voluntarily.
