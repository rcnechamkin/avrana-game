# Mission and Objective Model

## Mission

### LOCKED

The **Mission** is the public, fixed scenario goal.

Properties:

- public to all players;
- selected before play;
- does not change during the session;
- provides a stable shared reference point.

Standard scenario example:

> Ensure the ship reaches its destination.

## Primary Objective

### LOCKED

The **Primary Objective** is the participant's actual personal victory condition.

For most players at the start of a Standard game:

> Primary Objective: Ensure the ship reaches its destination.

This means Mission and Primary Objective are often identical at first, but remain distinct concepts.

## Divergent Primary Objectives

### PROVISIONAL

Some sessions may assign a player a divergent Primary Objective at setup.

Examples:

- Ensure Player 2 dies before arrival.
- Destroy the AI at all costs.
- Secure classified data and escape alive.
- Sabotage the ship.
- Preserve the AI on portable storage and escape.
- Ensure only you and the AI survive.
- Destroy the ship.

A divergent objective should have lore or in-world motivation. Avoid arbitrary "you are evil" framing.

## Objective changes during play

### LOCKED

A player's Primary Objective may change during play through a conscious choice.

Examples:

- accepting an AI bargain;
- accepting another player's formal recruitment;
- knowingly defecting from the Mission.

The UI should clearly warn the player when a choice changes their actual victory condition.

Example:

> If you accept, your Primary Objective becomes: ESCAPE WITH THE AI CORE.  
> The ship reaching its destination will no longer determine your victory.

## Agency rule

### LOCKED

Manipulation does not automatically equal objective change.

A player may be tricked into helping the AI while still retaining their original Primary Objective.

Distinguish:

- **Recruited / aligned:** player knowingly accepted a new goal or alliance.
- **Manipulated:** player unknowingly advanced another actor's goals while remaining loyal to their own.

## Secondary Objectives

### LOCKED

Secondary Objectives are meaningful optional goals.

They:

- matter to score and narrative;
- do not independently determine victory;
- can create additional tension.

Examples:

- Keep Player 3 alive.
- Preserve Engineering.
- Arrive with at least 40% fuel.
- Determine whether the AI lied.

## Tertiary Objectives

### LOCKED

Tertiary Objectives are smaller optional accomplishments.

Examples:

- Restore communications without AI help.
- Never use an escape pod.
- Repair three systems personally.
- Successfully verify a forged message.

## Objective count

### PROVISIONAL

Target:

- 1 Primary
- 0 to 2 Secondary
- 0 to 3 Tertiary

Avoid turning the game into a quest log.

## No guaranteed hostile human

### LOCKED

Possible session states include:

- all humans initially aligned;
- one divergent human;
- two divergent humans;
- initially aligned humans who later defect through choice.

Players should never know that a traitor must exist.

## Objective history

### PROVISIONAL

Maintain an internal objective/deal ledger.

This can support:

- post-game reconstruction;
- alliance history;
- commitment conflicts;
- score attribution;
- explaining why a player's incentives changed.

Example history:

- Start: Crew survives.
- Round 3: AI evacuation guarantee offered -> declined.
- Round 5: Alliance with Player 4 -> accepted.
- Round 7: AI survival pact -> accepted.

## Scenario vs. communication mode

### LOCKED

These are separate settings.

**Scenario / Game Type**
- defines the Mission;
- may add special scenario rules.

**Communication Mode**
- In-person;
- Remote / text-only.

## Standard Scenario

### LOCKED FOR MVP

Mission:

> Ensure the ship reaches its destination.

## Emissary Scenario

### FUTURE

Mission:

> Deliver the Emissary safely to the destination.

The ship arriving without the Emissary is a Mission Failure.

Treat as an advanced scenario, not MVP.
