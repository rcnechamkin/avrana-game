# Scoring & Endgame

## Victory — LOCKED

Personal victory is categorical.

A player wins if their **current Primary Objective** is satisfied at game end.

Multiple players can win.

## Score — LOCKED CONCEPT

Score is separate from victory.

Possible score sources — PROVISIONAL:

* Secondary Objectives
* Tertiary Objectives
* critical repairs
* efficient resource use
* rescues/protection
* investigations
* recruitment/alliance success
* survival/escape
* objective difficulty
* high-impact actions

A losing player may score higher than a winner.

The scoreboard ranks score. It does not decide who "won more."

## Results hierarchy — LOCKED

First results page should answer:

1. What happened to the public Mission?
2. Did I personally win or lose?
3. Did my Primary succeed?
4. Which Secondary Objectives succeeded?
5. Which Tertiary Objectives succeeded?
6. What was my score?
7. Where did I place on the score leaderboard?

## Post-game autopsy — LOCKED CONCEPT

A deeper results view can reveal:

* final objectives;
* objective history;
* AI motive profile;
* AI integrity problems;
* AI lies vs. sincere errors;
* recruitment/alliance history;
* system sabotage/access;
* deaths/escapes;
* major resource swings;
* timeline of turning points.

Quality target:

> "Damn, it all makes sense now."

Avoid:

> "The game randomly decided we lose."
