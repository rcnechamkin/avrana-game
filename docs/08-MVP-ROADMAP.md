# Prototype and MVP Roadmap

## Guiding rule

### LOCKED

Do not add social complexity until the underlying ship game proves fun.

## Prototype 0: cooperative ship puzzle

### GOAL

Prove that four loyal humans can have an interesting game.

### INCLUDE

- 4 players
- Standard Mission
- 4 to 6 ship systems
- 3 to 5 shared resources
- synchronous rounds
- simultaneous action commitment
- approximately 2 actions per player
- 8 to 10 rounds, provisional
- trustworthy/cooperative AI
- clear consequence forecasting

### EXCLUDE

- divergent human Primary Objectives
- AI deception
- recruitment
- message spoofing
- complex communication security
- escape pods
- advanced scenarios

### SUCCESS CRITERIA

Players must:

- disagree about priorities for rational reasons;
- make non-obvious tradeoffs;
- experience consequences from earlier choices;
- coordinate across specialties/resources;
- remain engaged throughout the round structure.

If perfect cooperation produces easy victory, revise the ship systems.

## Prototype 1: AI actor

### ADD

- AI recommendations;
- limited system control;
- self-preservation motive;
- explicit requests for authorization;
- AI actions that can help while creating new risk.

### TEST

Do players experience the AI as another participant with incentives, rather than a menu or event generator?

## Prototype 2: private information

### ADD

- asymmetric telemetry;
- private AI messages;
- limited AI integrity problems;
- evidence/diagnostic actions.

### TEST

Does uncertainty create useful discussion and deduction rather than confusion?

## Prototype 3: divergent objectives

### ADD

- occasional divergent Primary Objective at setup;
- no guaranteed traitor;
- lore-backed personal motives.

### TEST

Can the game support conflicting incentives without collapsing into traitor-hunting?

## Prototype 4: bargains and recruitment

### ADD

- AI offers;
- explicit Primary Objective changes;
- player-to-player recruitment;
- objective history.

### LATER WITHIN THIS STAGE

- false acceptance / false rejection;
- one-sided alliances.

## Prototype 5: communication warfare

### ADD

Primarily for Remote/Text mode:

- interception;
- spoofing;
- delayed messages;
- message loss;
- one-way channels;
- limited audit subsystem;
- stronger authentication/encryption gameplay.

## Post-MVP systems

### FUTURE

- Escape pods
- Emissary Scenario
- Cargo / Quarantine / Rescue scenarios
- richer AI motive templates
- more complex objective-generation rules
- post-death roles
- scenario packs
