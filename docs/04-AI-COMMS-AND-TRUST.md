# AI, Communications & Trust

## AI as fifth participant — LOCKED

The AI is an actor in the simulation with:

* motives;
* information;
* permissions;
* limitations;
* relationships;
* bargains.

It is not just narration or an event deck.

## Candidate AI motives — PROVISIONAL

* Mission success
* Self-preservation
* Crew survival
* Ship preservation
* Protect specific person/system/data/cargo
* Escape/freedom
* Contain another threat

## Motive vs. integrity — LOCKED

**Motive:** What does the AI want?

**Integrity:** How reliably can the AI perceive, remember, reason, and communicate?

Examples:

* high integrity + aligned = reliable ally;
* high integrity + misaligned = competent manipulator;
* low integrity + aligned = sincere but dangerous helper;
* low integrity + misaligned = chaotic adversary.

## Costly cooperation — LOCKED CONCEPT

The AI may sincerely want Mission success while valuing its own survival more than every individual human life.

## AI behavior — PROVISIONAL

AI may:

* recommend actions;
* diagnose systems;
* request authority;
* operate authorized systems;
* privately contact players;
* offer bargains;
* recruit;
* conceal information;
* selectively frame truth;
* lie when rules permit;
* make sincere mistakes.

## Human whispers — PROVISIONAL

Private channels can carry:

1. information;
2. proposals;
3. formal recruitment.

Formal recruitment can alter objectives if accepted.

## In-person mode — LOCKED

Players can always speak aloud.

Gameplay uncertainty comes from:

* telemetry;
* permissions;
* private objectives;
* system control;
* private digital messages;
* evidence quality.

## Remote/text-only mode — FUTURE/PROVISIONAL

Avrana controls the communication channel, enabling:

* delayed messages;
* dropped messages;
* interception;
* one-way links;
* spoofing;
* stale/replayed messages;
* altered messages.

## Security concepts — LOCKED CONCEPT

Translate to player language:

* Authentication -> Verified sender
* Encryption -> Private/exposed
* Integrity -> Message intact/may be altered
* Authorization -> Access allowed/denied
* Freshness -> Current/stale
* Auditability -> Recorded/unverified

Authentication proves who sent a message. It does **not** prove the message is true.

## False information — LOCKED

Distinguish:

* **Deception:** someone knowingly lied.
* **Corruption:** information was altered.
* **Error:** someone sincerely believed false information.

## Detectability — LOCKED

Uncertainty must be investigable.

Players need ways to improve confidence through:

* diagnostics;
* redundancy;
* logs;
* repairs;
* authentication;
* direct comparison;
* limited audit evidence.

## Trusted subsystem — PROVISIONAL

A black box / watchdog / flight recorder may verify narrow facts.

It should not become an oracle.
