# Decision Register

This file is a compact summary of decisions already made in discussion.

## LOCKED

- Avrana Game is developed independently from the Avrana Party device/appliance; later integration is not an MVP prerequisite.

- The game is phone-native.
- The game should exploit private screens and authoritative server state.
- The cooperative ship layer must be fun without betrayal.
- In-person players may always speak aloud.
- Remote/Text mode is distinct from In-person mode.
- Mission is public, fixed, and scenario-defined.
- Primary Objective is a player's actual victory condition.
- Mission and Primary are often identical at game start but remain separate concepts.
- Primary Objectives can sometimes diverge at setup.
- Primary Objectives can sometimes change through explicit player choice.
- A manipulated player is not automatically recruited.
- Human traitors are not guaranteed.
- AI motive and AI integrity are separate.
- Authentication proves sender identity, not truth.
- Deception must be investigable.
- Victory and score are separate.
- A losing player may outscore a winner.
- The first results page should be simple; deeper forensic detail belongs on a second page.
- Standard Scenario should be the MVP scenario.
- Advanced scenarios should not block the MVP.
- The AI does not need to be a generative model for the design to work.

## PROVISIONAL

- Synchronous round structure.
- Simultaneous action commitment.
- 2 actions per player per round.
- 8 to 10 rounds.
- 15 to 20 minute early prototype.
- 4 players.
- 4 to 6 core ship systems.
- 3 to 5 shared resources.
- Soft crew specialties.
- AI can request and receive subsystem authority.
- Objective history / deal ledger.
- Limited independent audit subsystem.
- Player-to-player recruitment.
- False acceptance/rejection as an advanced mechanic.
- Escape pods as a later system.

## FUTURE

- Emissary Scenario.
- Cargo Scenario.
- Quarantine Scenario.
- AI Core Scenario.
- Rescue Scenario.
- Deep remote-mode communication warfare.
- Post-death roles.
